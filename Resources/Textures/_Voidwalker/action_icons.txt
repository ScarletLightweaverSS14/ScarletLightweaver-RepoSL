Voidwalker action icons
Created: 2026-10-01
License: CC0-1.0

Original artwork created with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool, one generation per icon. No third-party reference images were used.
Each transparent source PNG was resized to 32x32 with System.Drawing, nearest-neighbor interpolation, PixelOffsetMode.Half, and SourceCopy compositing into Format32bppArgb. The entire source canvas was retained. This preserves the source alpha and avoids smoothing the pixel edges.
RSI: action_icons.rsi; six static, single-direction states. Volley and its temporary Launch Crystal action share the volley state.
