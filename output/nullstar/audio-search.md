# Nullstar — alternate audio sources

Verified source pages, 2026-10-04. These are audition candidates, not recordings imported into the current mix.

- [Beefy Explosions — SamsterBirdies / Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/SamsterBirdies/sounds/745549/): CC0, 18.145-second file containing bass-heavy explosions. A promising opening-impact candidate; choose an individual hit rather than using the entire sequence. Freesound requires login for the download.
- [Muffled Distant Explosion — NenadSimic / OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/muffled-distant-explosion): CC0; described as a low-pitched log-drum hit with delayed reverberation. Candidate for the distant pressure tail.
- [50 CC0 Sci-Fi SFX / OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/50-cc0-sci-fi-sfx): CC0 effects pack to audition for secondary spatial accents.
- [Impact Sounds / Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds): CC0, useful for stone/material accents; probably less suited to the main event on its own.

Useful search phrases on Freesound or OpenGameArt:

- cinematic explosion deep rumble long tail
- massive stone collapse debris rumble
- magical seal breaking dark impact
- sub bass drop pressure blast
- alien portal implosion resonance
- earthquake underground rumble
- crystal shatter pitched down reverb
- reverse whoosh cinematic build up

On Freesound select the **Creative Commons 0** license filter. CC0 permits modification and redistribution, including commercial use, without attribution requirements. CC BY recordings can also be useful when their attribution requirements are retained. Freesound carries several licenses; a free download alone does not identify the license. [Freesound license FAQ](https://freesound.org/help/faq/#licenses).

Suggested sequence: several seconds of rising stone/pressure during the last part of extraction, one decisive release impact synchronized to the shockwave, then 6–10 seconds of decaying rumble and sparse resonance. Avoid speech, music, and repeated explosive hits in the release tail.

The implemented revision uses existing repository recordings: vgstation explosions (CC-BY-SA-3.0), tgstation stone break, Baystation crystal accents, and original synthesis. Full runtime attribution remains beside unbound.wav. The preserved previous mix at source/unbound-previous.wav is CC-BY-SA-3.0, derived from tgstation break_stone, Baystation glass_crack1/2/3 and CM-SS13 metal_thud1 with original synthesized tones; see the previous stage package for its recipe and original attribution.
