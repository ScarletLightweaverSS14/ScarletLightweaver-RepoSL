Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production source artwork for a Space Station 14 pixel-art sword sprite, to be reduced to a 32 by 64 pixel item frame.
Primary request: Design Scarlet's sword NULLSTAR, an ancient sacred Void Tree weapon. One single complete sword only, upright, tip at top and pommel at bottom, perfectly front-facing orthographic view. Actual transparent background.
Shape: an imposing but elegant bastard-sword / greatsword hybrid, clearly slimmer and slightly smaller than an oversized slab sword. A long double-edged blade, modestly broad shoulders tapering to a precise lethal tip, an identifiable slightly asymmetric crystalline shoulder near the guard. Mostly continuous clean edges, not a jagged saw. The blade should look grown in sacred stone and torn free. Crafted dark crossguard with gently swept root-like quillons, long practical two-hand grip, small dark crystalline pommel. Guard and grip together take the bottom quarter; no massive ornament.
Materials: nearly black stone-metal, charcoal violet facets, restrained amethyst veins. Within the lower half of the blade, an elongated natural crystalline inclusion holds a very small pale-violet star-like point. The inclusion looks like trapped distant starlight, not a battery. Sparse irregular violet veins climb toward the tip. Highlights articulate the cutting edge without making the entire sword glowing neon. Old and prestigious, alien but cleanly readable.
Style: crisp authentic low-resolution pixel art, large deliberate pixel clusters, limited 12-16 color palette, sharp stepped pixels, simple strong shading. Design as if a roughly 50-pixel-tall sword was enlarged using nearest neighbor. Prioritize readability at normal SS14 game zoom. Full weapon occupies most of image height, comfortable clear transparent margins on every side.
Lighting: the core is quiet/static for this sprite phase; a tiny star-like inclusion is the recognizable detail. No animation frames.
Avoid: Hadal Blade silhouette, enormous slab proportions, broad serrated edge, industrial parts, vents, tubes, machinery, sci-fi energy swords, guns, character, hands, scenery, sword stone, floating particles, mist, outer bloom, floor shadow, text, lettering, labels, panels, sprite sheet, extra weapons, existing cosmic-cult weapon designs. No anti-aliasing or painterly gradients.
